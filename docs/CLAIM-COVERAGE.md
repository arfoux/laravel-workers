# Claim coverage — `laravel-workers` 0.5.1

Scope: every published claim in `README.md` lines 24–94 (What this is, counts, commands, What runs today, refused constructs, Honest limits), the Philosophy section (`README.md:154–159`, especially "Coverage enforced"), plus `package.json` scripts. S146k(1) flagged Philosophy as an unscoped claim family that collided with P7; it is now in scope and row 36 records that claim as REFUTED for published 0.5.1 (the local fix is unreleased). The only claim family deliberately excluded is a **fresh** Cloudflare Workers CPU benchmark (user decision; see "Out of scope"). No numbers are estimated here: every figure below comes from an observed command result or a named file/evidence section.

## What "proven" means — four evidence tiers

| Tier | Name | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Build gate** | Reproducible local/CI command (`npm run gate:ci`, `build --strict`, `tools/construct-audit.mjs`, `tools/docs-check.mjs`, `tools/pack-gate.mjs`) that fails on a wrong answer. Shows the compiler refuses or classifies a construct; does not show deployed behaviour. |
| T2 | **Unit / integration test** | `tools/tests/`, `kernel/tests/`, `shadow/scenarios/` cases that exercise a method, route or handler directly against a stub or local harness. |
| T3 | **PHP-vs-compiled shadow** | The real Laravel app served by PHP compared with the compiled worker (`node shadow/run.mjs --app <root> --scenarios shadow/scenarios/lw-showcase.mjs`), including byte-identical view output. |
| T4 | **Deployed app HTTP** | Real HTTP requests to the 0.5.1 Worker deployed on Cloudflare, recorded in `docs/EVIDENCE.md` section "Verified: the four published fixes on the live app (2026-11-16)". |

A verdict of **PROVEN** means at least one tier above actually exercised the claim; **PARTIAL** means the claim is documented and covered for a subset (typically T1/T2, or T4 for one representative route) but not across its full stated surface; **NOT VERIFIED** means no listed tier produced evidence for it in this pass.
